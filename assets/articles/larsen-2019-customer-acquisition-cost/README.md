# Larsen: acquisition cost and incrementality

Kim Larsen (May 14, 2019). **You Don't Know Your Customer Acquisition Cost.** Noteworthy / The Journal Blog.

[Original article](https://blog.usejournal.com/you-dont-know-your-customer-acquisition-cost-f7bf820f9d4a).

This is the assigned MBA 743 Topic 3 reading. Its five-page course-distributed PDF was used to verify the author's distinction between attributed acquisitions, background inflow, and incremental and marginal acquisition costs. This folder records the source and its role; it does not redistribute the article or course PDF. No public reprint permission is asserted.

Studio North's acquisition tests, calculations and uncertainty intervals are original implementations. The article's arm labels differ from the lab's convention: the lab calls campaign exposure treatment and withholding control. The relevant comparison is the same focal-marketing counterfactual.

See [CAC and LTV methodology](../../../docs/cac-incrementality.md) and the report's Section 8 for the numerical reconciliation and its limits. The separately bundled Orduz notebook has its own MIT license and provenance; that permission does not apply to Larsen's article.
