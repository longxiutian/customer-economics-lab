# Release 1.4: management report and customer segmentation

The historical labels in this note use the original numbering. See [the release mapping](versioning.md) for the 2026 1.x series and preserved original tags.

The updated observed dataset supports clearer differences between baseline customer groups while retaining variation within each group and between successive joining cohorts. The analysis still begins with eight separate CSV exports; generation and the future evaluation outcomes are outside the student workflow.

## Report changes

- One broad business experience map introduces the membership and possible friction. Separate segment journey maps have been removed.
- The observed-versus-forecast retention exhibit now appears near the beginning, after a plain-language explanation of extrapolation.
- Dedicated separator pages introduce the business, forecast purpose, cohort economics, trajectories, segmentation, company economics, valuation, campaigns and appendix.
- Segmentation findings lead with percentage points and dollars. The comparison of four two-group strategies uses identical customers and outcome windows; variance diagnostics are in the appendix.
- Retention-table numerals stay dark. Cash-recovery numerals are red below zero and dark green at or above zero. An outline identifies the first observed recovery of CAC; future cells remain blank.
- All figures and tables have numbered captions and automatic cross-references.
